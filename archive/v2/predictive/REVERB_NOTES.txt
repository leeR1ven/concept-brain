REVERBERATING PFC HOLD — parameter map (draft notes for the inline report)

Setup (reverb_sweep.py): N 2048, leaky k-WTA, learned recurrence by PR (state target), fan-in 1000; inputs = the 147
realistic bench line codes (8-unit unigram + bigram codes, frame chars shared; mean pairwise input Jaccard 0.06-0.09),
heard with a Zipf frequency; 1,500 training lines; weak random distractors during holds; 51 settings mapped.

Why M6 collapsed (mechanism, SIMULATED):
1. learning on the first tick of a load, whose source is the OLD thought -> every line wired to the one before; over
   1,500 random pairs everything links to the shared/frequent units = one generic basin.  Removing only this:
   decode 0.03 -> 0.77 at 60 ticks.
2. more active units than the line drives (k 120 vs ~59): free slots are filled by what the old thought / frequent
   lines predict -> mixing.  k ~ line size (40-60) brings held-state overlap down to the inputs' own (0.05-0.11).
3. a fast set point (tau 500) suppresses the units being held -> no hold (M6b).  A slow one (tau 20,000) only helps
   once 1 and 2 are fixed.
4. learning WHILE a line is heard (old and new mixed) collapses the continuous regime too (J60 0.54-1.00); learning in
   the settled state after the input (settle-learning) does not.

Gated regime (reset + load; the designer rejects it, kept for reference): k40 + frame-feature normalisation: decode
0.97/0.97/0.87/0.80 at 10/30/60/120, overlap 0.05 (inputs 0.06), replacement +0.00, period-2 0; long holds stay (0.92
at 500); favourites only with free slots (k120: 67 % of long silences end near top-10 % lines, but overlap 0.36).

Continuous regime (no reset; the line flows in char by char; others' speech enters strongly, own speech not):
  collapse:   amp <= 2, or learning while hearing, or k >> line size
  sticky:     leak 0.9 with amp 2-4 -> takeover 0.2-0.6 (the old thought's potential ~10x its drive wins)
  balanced:   leak 0.8, amp 4-6, k ~ line size, settle-learning -> takeover 0.83-0.97 at 3 ticks, hold >= 0.83 for
              120 ticks, line n-1 kept as context 0.10-0.27 (chance 0.05), overlap ~ inputs, period-2 ~ 0
  favourites: + fast fatigue (0.2, tau 50) -> 33 % of 500-tick silences end near top-10 % frequent lines (chance 10 %),
              hold 0.80 at 60, takeover 0.67
Recommended: k 60, amp 4, leak 0.8, fatigue 0.1/100, settle-learning (first 10 silent ticks): takeover 0.87/0.87/0.87
(3/6/10 ticks after the line), context n-1 0.27/0.27/0.23, hold 0.83/0.83/0.83 (30/60/120), overlap 0.09 (inputs 0.06),
J(60,500) 0.94, period-2 -0.001.
